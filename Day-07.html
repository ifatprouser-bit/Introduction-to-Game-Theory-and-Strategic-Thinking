Of the five past papers, this one asks for the most arithmetic. It has no commitment questions, because that topic was first taught in 2025. So it is a clean test of trees, dominance, pure Nash and mixed strategies.

How to sit this one

90 minutes, 14 questions, five options each, in the paper's own order. Press Start and don't stop the clock for anything except a real interruption.

  • Nothing is shown while you work. Clicking an option only records your choice. No green, no red, no explanation. That is on purpose. On the day, nobody tells you.
  • Answer every question. The paper states no penalty for a wrong answer. So a blank scores zero for certain, and a guess might not.
  • Work in two passes. Answer everything you can in about 50 minutes. Then go back to the three or four hard ones.
  • Read the rounding instruction. This paper says "closest percentage point" in one place and "two digits after the decimal point" in another.

Press Submit when you are done, or let the clock run to zero. The page then submits by itself. The explanations open only then.
