Title: Day 9 — The small print, and salami tactics
Voices: NOA = Heart, TOM = Fenrir
Output: Day-09-small-print.mp3

NOA: Every one of the eight has a catch, and the exam likes the catch more than the device.
TOM: Start with contracts.
NOA: A contract has to survive being renegotiated. If, on the day, both of us would rather tear it up, then it was never a commitment. It was a wish. And it needs somebody outside the two of us to enforce it. Where courts are slow or corrupt, people go looking for other enforcers, and that gets ugly quickly.
TOM: And cutting off communication? That one looked free.
NOA: It is not. You pay twice. You lose flexibility, because you cannot react to news you never hear. And you lose any way of checking, because you cannot even confirm your message arrived.
TOM: What about leaving the outcome beyond my control? That is the one that frightens me.
NOA: It is a balance, and the professor chooses that word carefully. The device must be strong enough that the other side is genuinely deterred. But not so strong that the first false alarm ends everything. And false alarms are not a possibility. Given enough time they are a certainty.
TOM: So the more automatic I make it, the better it deters and the worse it fails.
NOA: That is the trade in one sentence. Now small steps. They work only while there is no obvious final step. The moment we can both see the last payment coming, we both start reasoning backwards from it, and the trust unravels from the end.
TOM: So leave the door open to more business afterwards.
NOA: And now the mirror. Small steps run in reverse. Look at the picture on the page. A child is told not to go in the water. He sits on the bank. Then his toes. Then he stands up, still only feet in. Then a little deeper, and straight back out.
TOM: And no single one of those is worth having a fight about.
NOA: Until he is swimming out of sight. That is salami tactics. Slicing somebody else's commitment so thin that they never find a slice worth defending. Same tool as small steps, pointed the other way.
