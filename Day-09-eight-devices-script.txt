Title: Day 9 — The eight devices and their eight pictures
Voices: NOA = Heart, TOM = Fenrir
Output: Day-09-eight-devices.mp3

NOA: Eight of them, and the professor teaches them in a fixed order. Keep his order. The order is free memory.
TOM: Give me pictures, not definitions.
NOA: One. Contracts. The picture is the NATO treaty, the clause saying an attack on any one of us is an attack on all of us. Written down, and enforced by somebody other than me.
TOM: So on the day, the punishment is no longer my decision.
NOA: Two. Reputation. Kennedy, in public, during the Berlin crisis. If we do not keep our word here, he said, everything we have promised anywhere means nothing. He was not really talking about Berlin.
TOM: He was talking about every crisis after it. And a strong enough reputation does the work of a contract with nothing signed.
NOA: Three. Cutting off communication. Doctor Strangelove. A base commander sends the bombers and then destroys the radio, so nobody can recall them. Including himself.
TOM: You cannot be talked out of it if you cannot be reached.
NOA: Four. Burning bridges behind you. William the Conqueror lands in England and burns his own ships. No way home, so there is nothing left to argue about.
TOM: And the submarine captain in The Hunt for Red October, who posts a letter announcing his defection before he sails. Same trick. He has made turning back fatal.
NOA: Five. Leaving the outcome beyond your control. The doomsday machine, from the same film. A machine that answers automatically and cannot be switched off, because a machine that could be switched off would be switched off.
TOM: And six?
NOA: Six. Moving in small steps. You do not trust the builder, the builder does not trust you, so nobody hands over everything at once. You pay a little as each piece of the kitchen is finished.
TOM: One big frightening promise, chopped into a chain of small safe ones.
NOA: Seven. Teamwork. The Roman army. Running from a battle was punished by death. And failing to kill the man beside you who ran was also punished by death.
TOM: So nobody can quietly look away. The group holds itself.
NOA: Eight. Mandated negotiating agents. A worker who says he cannot take less than a certain raise is not believed, because why would he refuse slightly less? So he hands the talking to a union leader who has taken a narrow mandate in public and would lose everything by bending.
TOM: Weakness on purpose. I cannot give you what you want, because I am not allowed to.
NOA: Look at the eight small cards on the page, and say the names out loud in order once. Contracts, reputation, cutting off communication, burning bridges, beyond your control, small steps, teamwork, mandated agents.
