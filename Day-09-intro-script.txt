Title: Day 9 — Making strategies credible
Voices: NOA = Heart, TOM = Fenrir
Output: Day-09-intro.mp3

NOA: Today there is nothing to calculate. Not one number anywhere. This part of the course is pure memory.
TOM: So what am I meant to walk away with?
NOA: Eight names, and eight pictures. That is genuinely all of it. The exam hands you a small story and asks which of the eight things is happening in it.
TOM: Then let me guess where it goes wrong. Not the stories. Remembering which name belongs to which story.
NOA: Exactly. So we do not start with the list. We start with the problem the list was invented to solve. Look at the first tree on the page. A big firm tells a newcomer, come in here and I will start a price war. And the newcomer walks straight in.
TOM: Because a price war would hurt the big firm too. Everyone can see that.
NOA: That is the whole disease in one line. A threat that would hurt you to carry out is a threat nobody believes.
TOM: So the cure is to make it hurt less?
NOA: The cure is stranger than that. The cure is to take away your own freedom to back down. Every one of the eight devices is a different way of doing that.
TOM: Giving up my own options on purpose. That sounds like a mistake.
NOA: Which is exactly why it earns a whole lecture.
